Campus coupe

Adapted from a public-domain concept car by Unity Fan.

Campus adaptation includes simplified meshes at two levels of detail, reset wheel
poses, vertex ambient occlusion, new paint and materials, custom instruments and
number plate, damage deformation, and controls for steering, suspension and lights.
This adaptation omits the original brand artwork.

Urban Courtyard 02 reflection environment from Poly Haven, resized and filtered.
